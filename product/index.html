Can I buy a BioSense Tag today?

BioSense is being developed with farmers and partners. Contact us to discuss pilot participation, product availability and what deployment could look like for your herd.

Will I need a smartphone?

The BioSense experience is being designed for both smartphones and feature phones, with app, SMS and USSD access. The channels available in a pilot will be confirmed with participating farmers.

How does the tag connect in the field?

The proposed design combines LoRa gateway connectivity with Bluetooth Low Energy. Coverage depends on the gateway setup, terrain and local conditions, which are assessed during pilot planning.

Are the specifications final?

The figures shown here are design targets for the current product concept. Battery life, weight, range, materials and environmental protection remain subject to field validation and final product development.
